      How the numbers work

      What is included

      • Every event the trackers hold: each series listed on the home page (watched live), and every Triton festival since 2018 (Triton publishes its full history). Each series' own page explains how its bullets and prizes are counted; this page adds them up.
      • Amounts are converted to one currency at the rate of each date: a buy-in at the event's start, a prize at its finish (European Central Bank rates; TWD and VND from a daily open feed).

      Matching players across series

      • The series don't share player ids, so a player is matched by name, in any word order and ignoring accents and hyphens ("Chen Kuan-Han" at Triton is "Kuan Han Chen" at APT), and only when the countries agree (or one series has none).
      • Two different players with the same name and country count as one, and a player whose name is spelt differently in two series shows up twice. Common short names are the most likely to be merged wrongly.

      Filters

      • "Finished events only" leaves out events still running. "All bullets known" keeps only events where every bullet is credited to a player, using each series' own rule. Without it, totals that include other events are a minimum (≥).
