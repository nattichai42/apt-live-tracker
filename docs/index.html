          How the numbers work

          Where the data comes from

          • This page reads the APT site's live seating for every running event, from your browser (every 60 s by default). A player whose seat disappears is logged as busted; a busted player who sits down again while registration is open is a re-entry.
          • A poller on GitHub does the same around the clock and saves its record every few minutes. This page loads that record on open, then continues live.
          • Payouts (places and prizes) come from the site's payout pages, checked every minute while an event is in the money. Chip counts on the site are not live, so they are not shown.
          • Times are Taipei time. A bust is stamped at the poll that first saw the seat empty.

          Event cards

          • Entries (official) = players seen + re-entries + waiting for a seat + missed. The coloured bar shows that split.
          • Waiting for a seat: registered (counted in entries and players left) but not in the seating list yet.
          • Missed bullets were never seen: busted before tracking began, a re-entry bought at the table without leaving the seat (the seating list doesn't change and the site has no live chip counts), or a bust and re-entry between two polls.
          • A return counts as a re-entry only while registration is open and the official entry count goes up since the bust. The count often updates after the seating list, so the tracker waits up to 3 minutes for it; if it doesn't rise, the bust is undone as a data glitch. The seat doesn't matter: a rebuy can land back in the same seat.
          • A later day (Day 2, Final Day) shows players carried over from the flights, plus new entries if registration is open.
          • "Over" means we counted more bullets than the official entries: registrations cancelled after seating.
          • For a later day (Day 2, Final Day) the entries cover every flight, so missed is only known when all flights were watched from their start.

          Players

          • Buy-in = bullets seen × buy-in. A ≥ means some of that player's events were not watched from the start; there one bullet is counted.
          • Events played before tracking began only show their cashers, so ITM and net look far better than reality. Tick "Watched from start only" for honest totals.
          • Players are matched by name; name edits by the floor (e.g. reordered names) are merged.
