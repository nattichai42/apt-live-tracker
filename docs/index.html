      How this works and its limits
      • Every 60 seconds (by default) this page reads the APT site's live seating for every running event, straight from your browser. A player whose seat disappears is logged as busted; a busted player who sits down again is a re-entry.
      • History comes from a poller on GitHub Actions that does the same thing around the clock and saves its record every few minutes. This page loads that record on open and then continues live.
      • Times are Taipei time. A bust is stamped at the poll that first saw the seat empty, so it can be up to one poll late.
      • A bust and re-entry inside one poll interval looks like a seat change and is missed. Those show up as untracked bullets on the event card.
      • Results (places and prizes) come from the site's payout pages. Players are matched by name exactly as printed.
      • Chip counts are not live on the site, so they are not shown.
